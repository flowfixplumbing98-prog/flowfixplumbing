Common Faucet Problems We Fix

Constant dripping: usually a worn cartridge or washer — a 30-minute fix in most cases. Low water pressure at one tap: often a clogged aerator or a failing shut-off valve. Stiff or squeaky handles: mineral buildup or a dry O-ring. Leaking at the base: failed seals that can rot the cabinet below if ignored. If you see water where it shouldn't be, don't wait — small faucet leaks become expensive cabinet and flooring repairs.
